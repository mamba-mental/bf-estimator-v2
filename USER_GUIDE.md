## Smart Analysis Features

### Algorithm-based Progress Analysis

The Smart Analysis engine uses statistical algorithms to analyze your progress data, identify patterns, and provide personalized recommendations.

**How to use:**
1. Navigate to the "Weekly Progress" tab
2. Enter at least 4 weeks of consistent data to receive meaningful analysis
3. Click the "Analyze" button to generate insights
4. Review the trends, patterns, and recommendations in the "Smart Analysis" section

### Plateau Detection

When your progress stalls, the system automatically detects plateaus and provides recommendations to help you break through.

**How to use:**
1. Continue entering weekly data consistently
2. The system will automatically detect if your measurements plateau (less than 0.5 change over 4 weeks)
3. When a plateau is detected, you'll see a notification with recommendations
4. Follow the suggested adjustments to overcome plateaus

### Adaptive Goal Adjustments

The system analyzes your progress and suggests goal adjustments based on your actual results.

**How to use:**
1. Set your initial goals (weight, body fat percentage)
2. As you track progress, the system will analyze your actual results
3. If your rate of progress differs from what's expected, you'll receive suggested goal adjustments
4. You can accept or modify these suggested goals

## Expanded Metrics

### Additional Body Measurements

Beyond weight and body fat, you can now track various body measurements to get a complete picture of your progress.

**How to use:**
1. Navigate to the "Weekly Progress" tab
2. Select the "Measurements" sub-tab
3. Enter values for any of the following measurements:
   - Waist circumference
   - Hip measurements
   - Chest measurements
   - Arm circumference
   - Thigh measurements
   - Neck circumference
4. Click "Save" to store your measurements
5. View progress graphs for each measurement type

**Tips:**
- Measure at the same time of day for consistency
- Use the same measurement technique each time
- Record measurements weekly for best results

## Customizable Dashboard

The dashboard provides a personalized view of your most important metrics.

**How to use:**
1. Navigate to the "Dashboard" tab
2. Click "Add Widget" to add a new widget to your dashboard
3. Select from available widget types:
   - Weight Progress
   - Body Fat Progress
   - Measurements
   - Smart Analysis
4. Each widget has a "Move" button to rearrange its position
5. Click the "✕" button to remove a widget

**Widget Types:**
- **Weight Progress**: Shows your weight changes over time
- **Body Fat Progress**: Displays body fat percentage changes
- **Measurements**: Shows changes in a selected body measurement
- **Smart Analysis**: Provides algorithm-based insights and recommendations

## Theme Options

The application now supports multiple theme options beyond the standard light/dark modes.

**How to use:**
1. Navigate to the "About" tab
2. Find the "Enhanced Themes" section
3. Click on a theme name to apply it:
   - Midnight: Dark blue with subtle accents
   - Forest: Green-focused theme with natural tones
   - Lavender: Purple-based calming theme
   - Sunset: Warm orange and red gradients
4. The theme is applied immediately

## Voice Input

Voice commands allow for hands-free data entry.

**How to use:**
1. Navigate to the "Weekly Progress" tab
2. Click the "Voice Input" button
3. Speak your measurement clearly, for example:
   - "Weight 180 pounds"
   - "Body fat 18 percent"
   - "Waist 34 inches"
4. The system will process your voice command and fill in the appropriate field
5. Verify the entry is correct and save your data

**Supported Commands:**
- "Weight [number] pounds"
- "Body fat [number] percent"
- "Waist/Hips/Chest/Arms/Thighs/Neck [number] inches"

## Date Format Notes

When entering dates, the following formats are supported:
- MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 03/27/2025)
- MMDDYY (e.g., 032725)
- MM-DD-YYYY (e.g., 03-27-2025)

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues:

**Issue**: "Failed to generate report: time data does not match format"
**Solution**: Ensure dates are entered in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 03/27/2025) or MMDDYY format (e.g., 032725)

**Issue**: Voice input not working
**Solution**: 
- Ensure your microphone is connected and working
- Speak clearly and at a normal pace
- Use the exact phrase format listed in the Voice Input section

**Issue**: Dashboard widgets not appearing
**Solution**: Ensure you have sufficient data entered (at least 1-2 weeks) for widgets to display meaningful information

**Issue**: Blank or transparent UI elements
**Solution**: Try changing the theme in the About tab, then change back to your preferred theme

## Getting Started with Enhanced Features

For the best experience with the enhanced Body Fat Estimator:

1. First, enter your personal information in the "Input Data" tab
2. Begin tracking your weekly progress data consistently
3. After 4 weeks of data, explore the Smart Analysis features
4. Customize your dashboard with the widgets most relevant to you
5. Experiment with different themes to find your preferred look
6. Use voice input for quick data entry during your weekly updates

Remember that many insights and recommendations become more accurate with more data, so consistent tracking is key to getting the most value from the enhanced features.
